# External-validation decision

Decision date: **2026-10-21**

## Decision

The current manuscript will be submitted as a ChEMBL 37 chemical-OOD benchmark
with temporal validation. It will **not** claim independent external validation.
No performance score was calculated on an external subset that failed the
prespecified eligibility gate.

## BindingDB audit

The October 2026 BindingDB article-curated subset was chosen because it separates
BindingDB literature curation from the ChEMBL- and PubChem-sourced subsets. The
downloaded archive had vendor MD5
`4b1d2f7c90e83dc6fd13e7b106a1f545` and local SHA-256
`727383bac35beed6bc5c55c40dfbd58ba023fa97a7f27dc1a61440c1ad54a2b7`.

The locked filter required:

- exact human UniProt target P27487 (DPP4), P22303 (AChE), or P35354 (PTGS2);
- a single protein chain and exact numeric Ki or IC50;
- active pChEMBL >= 6 or inactive pChEMBL <= 5, excluding the grey zone;
- a DOI or PMID absent from the ChEMBL 37 activity extraction;
- no standardized molecule present anywhere in the ChEMBL 37 modelling cohort;
- maximum radius-2 Morgan Tanimoto strictly below 0.70 to that cohort; and
- at least 30 active and 30 inactive pairs for every target.

Of 1,908 target-matched rows, 1,340 strict binary measurements remained before
provenance filtering. Document exclusion left 459 measurements. Contradiction,
exact-molecule, and similarity filtering produced 233 final pairs:

| Target | Active | Inactive | Eligible |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| DPP4 | 35 | 23 | No |
| AChE | 32 | 87 | Yes |
| PTGS2/COX-2 | 17 | 39 | No |

Only AChE passed the class-count threshold, so the candidate source is
insufficient for a confirmatory three-target external evaluation. Reporting the
AChE subset alone after seeing target availability would change the scope and
would not answer the manuscript's three-target claim.

The complete machine-readable gate result is
[`docs/manifests/bindingdb_external_audit.json`](manifests/bindingdb_external_audit.json).
The audit is regenerated by `scripts/audit_bindingdb_external.py`; raw BindingDB
rows and the candidate Parquet file are intentionally not part of the compact
repository release.

## Interpretation and next route

MoleculeACE is not suitable because the required targets are absent and the
benchmark is ChEMBL-derived. PubChem BioAssay is a primary archive whose
contributor-specific licences and overlap would require assay-by-assay review.
GtoPdb is useful curated pharmacology data, but its current access and overlap
constraints do not establish a sufficiently powered, three-target independent
cohort here.

The scientifically valid next extension is either a prospectively locked,
source- and document-disjoint dataset that passes the same eligibility gate, or
a prospective biochemical assay with appropriate positive, negative,
interference, and purity controls. This extension is desirable but is not
represented as completed in the current paper.
